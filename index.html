CRO Page Prioritization

Where the Conversion
Leaks Are

The most valuable conversion mechanism on this site is the finance lookup flow, where users search for a ticker and land on a finance page. The flow is bleeding value because the search inputs are ambiguous or unlabeled, and the finance pages lack trust signals, causing users to hesitate or abandon. For example, the homepage search box shows three stacked placeholder lines with similar phrasing, and the ticker input on finance pages has no visible label, while pages like /finance/NVDA show no reviews, guarantees, or security badges near the price and analyst consensus.
